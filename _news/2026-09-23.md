Excited to share that our review on "The next generation of fear generalization" is now in press in [Trends in Cognitive Science](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1364661326002068)!. A collaboration led by Sam Cooper [(SCOOP lab)](https://www.scoop-lab.com/home), we review human and animal neuroscience of fear generalization and offer suggestions for future translational research.
